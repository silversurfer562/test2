## Context

What forces this decision now. Constraints, scale assumptions, time pressure.
Enough that a reader in a year knows what you were up against — most of the
value of an old ADR is in this section.

## Decision

What you are doing. Present tense, active. One paragraph.

## Alternatives rejected

The section that makes this a decision record rather than a description.

- **[Alternative]** — why not. Attach a measurement where one exists.
- **[Alternative]** — why not.
- **[Do nothing]** — always consider it explicitly. Sometimes it wins.

If you cannot name a rejected alternative, you have not made a decision yet —
you have described what happened. Go find the alternative, or mark this
record incomplete and say so.

## Consequences

- What gets better, with numbers where you have them
- **What gets worse.** Every decision has this section. A record without it is
  advocacy.
- What becomes harder to change later

## Revisit when

Conditions, not dates. "If p95 exceeds 4s" or "if we exceed 10k memories per
user." Written now, while you still remember what would have changed your
mind.

## Evidence

Links to the eval run, the trace, the benchmark. The numbers this rests on.
